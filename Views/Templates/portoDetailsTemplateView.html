Challenge

Chamelure is an innovative eye therapy app designed to help children with amblyopia (lazy eye) engage in fun, interactive treatments that support vision improvement. Using adaptive and visually captivating experiences, Chamelure provides an alternative to traditional eye patch therapy, making treatment more enjoyable and less stigmatizing for children.
