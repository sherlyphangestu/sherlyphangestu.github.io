Outline

With TuneUp, tuning your ukulele has never been easier or more precise—whether you're at home, on stage, or practicing on the go. Available on your Apple Watch, the app uses advanced signal processing and FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) to ensure each string is tuned to the perfect pitch. Through a user-friendly interface and intuitive wrist-based feedback, subtle haptic vibrations guide you throughout the process, signaling when adjustments are needed and confirming when the perfect pitch is achieved. This seamless combination of convenience and accuracy makes tuning intuitive and effortless for musicians of all levels.
